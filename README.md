# Rock Paper Scissors Game (C)

A simple command-line Rock Paper Scissors game written in C. Play against 
the computer, track your score across rounds, and see a final leaderboard 
when you're done.

## Features
- Classic Rock, Paper, Scissors gameplay
- Random computer choice using C's rand() function
- Score tracking for both player and computer
- Play multiple rounds in a single session
- Final leaderboard showing overall winner

## How to Play
1. Choose Rock (1), Paper (2), or Scissors (3)
2. The computer randomly picks its move
3. The winner of each round is displayed
4. Choose to play again or quit
5. View your final score and overall winner
